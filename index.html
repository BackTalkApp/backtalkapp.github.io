Listen to anything.

Highlight text in any Windows app. Backtalk reads it aloud in a voice you picked, at a speed you set, on your own machine. Nothing you read is sent anywhere.

It runs on your computer

Backtalk has no server. The voices, the reading and the translation all happen on the machine in front of you. That is not a setting you turn on. There is nowhere else for any of it to go.

Your text stays put

Text you highlight is read by a voice engine running on your own machine. It is never uploaded, never queued for processing somewhere else, and never logged off the device.

No account, ever

No sign-in, no email, no API key, no licence server. Install it and it works. Backtalk does not know who you are and has no way to find out.

No telemetry

Nothing is counted, timed or reported. There is no analytics, no crash reporting and no usage data, because there is no service collecting it.

Translation is offline too

Language packs are downloaded once during install and then live on your disk. Translating a sentence does not touch the network.

Your files, your folders

Reading history and exported audio are ordinary files in folders you choose in Settings. Move them, back them up, or delete them. It is your disk.

Works with the internet off

After install, unplug it. Everything except downloading a new language pack keeps working exactly the same.

None of that has to be taken on trust. The source is public, so you can read the network code yourself, or watch it with any traffic monitor you like.

What it does

The full specification.

Reading

  • Highlight to read, from any application
  • Typed and pasted text
  • Auto-speak, on or off
  • Playback speed, adjustable mid-sentence
  • Skip back and forward ten seconds
  • Scrub bar with elapsed time
  • Volume control
  • WAV export

Voices

  • 55 voices
  • 8 languages
  • Browse one language at a time
  • Voice type and pitch listed for every voice
  • Star a voice to pin it to the top
  • Tick the voices you want in the Reader
  • Double-click to hear a voice before choosing it

Translation

  • Offline translation, 7 languages
  • Pick a voice, hear your text in that language
  • Original and translation shown together
  • Read as written, leaving foreign text in its own language
  • Automatic voice switching by detected language

The application

  • Compact player
  • Reading history, sortable and replayable
  • Light and dark themes
  • Chooseable storage locations
  • Windows installer, no command line

55 voices, 8 languages

Every voice is installed with the app. Translation into the seven non-English languages is optional and you pick which ones during install.

  • English 29 voices
  • Chinese 8 voices
  • Japanese 5 voices
  • Hindi 4 voices
  • Spanish 3 voices
  • Portuguese 3 voices
  • Italian 2 voices
  • French 1 voice

Windows will warn you about this file. Here is why.

Backtalk is not code-signed yet. A signing certificate costs a few hundred dollars a year, and this is a free application, so for now it ships without one.

When you run the installer, Windows shows a blue box saying "Windows protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway. That warning means the file is unsigned. It does not mean anything was found in it.

If you would rather check for yourself, here is the SHA-256 of Backtalk-Setup.exe. Compare it against your download with certutil -hashfile Backtalk-Setup.exe SHA256.
